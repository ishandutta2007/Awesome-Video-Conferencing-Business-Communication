# Awesome-Video-Conferencing-Business-Communication

## Top Video Conferencing & Business Communication Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Video Meetings, Team Messaging & Self-Hosted Communication Platforms*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial video conferencing platforms** and **open-source projects** that enable real-time meetings, webinars, team messaging, and business communication. These tools range from browser-based meeting solutions to full unified communications suites with telephony and chat.



**Examples** include Amazon Chime, Zoom Workplace, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Cisco Webex, GoTo Meeting, BlueJeans, RingCentral Video, Dialpad Meetings, and Whereby (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Video conferencing is one of the strongest open-source domains. **Jitsi Meet** leads as the most widely deployed open-source video platform with 25,000+ GitHub stars. **BigBlueButton** dominates education with LMS integration. **La Suite Meet** brings modern LiveKit-powered conferencing from the French government. **Element (Matrix)** provides secure messaging with video, and **Nextcloud Talk** integrates conferencing with file collaboration. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Zoom Workplace](https://zoom.us/)**  

  **The dominant video conferencing platform** — meetings, webinars, phone, chat, and AI Companion. **Free tier limits group meetings to 40 minutes**; paid from $14.99/month. **The reference for video meetings** .



- **[Microsoft Teams](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-teams/)**  

  **Enterprise collaboration hub** — chat, video meetings, calling, and Office 365 integration. **Bundled with Microsoft 365** — the default for Microsoft-centric organizations .



- **[Google Meet](https://meet.google.com/)**  

  **Browser-based video conferencing integrated with Google Workspace** . **The simplest video meeting experience** — no downloads required, free tier limits group calls to 60 minutes .



- **[Cisco Webex](https://www.webex.com/)**  

  **Enterprise UC suite** — meetings, calling, messaging, and contact center. **Strong in regulated industries** with compliance certifications .



- **[Amazon Chime](https://aws.amazon.com/chime/)**  

  AWS's video conferencing and business communication service. **Best for AWS-centric organizations** .



- **[GoTo Meeting](https://www.goto.com/meeting)**  

  **The veteran video conferencing platform** — simple, reliable meetings with screen sharing. **Best for straightforward meeting needs** .



- **[BlueJeans](https://www.bluejeans.com/)**  

  Verizon's video conferencing platform (discontinued for new customers) — **historically significant** .



- **[RingCentral Video](https://www.ringcentral.com/)**  

  **Unified communications with video meetings** — messaging, video, and phone in one platform. **Best for organizations replacing traditional PBX** .



- **[Dialpad Meetings](https://www.dialpad.com/)**  

  **AI-powered video meetings** with real-time transcription and sentiment analysis. **Best for sales and customer-facing teams** .



- **[Whereby](https://whereby.com/)**  

  **Browser-based video meetings** — no downloads, embeddable in websites. **Best for customer-facing video** .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Jitsi Meet](https://github.com/jitsi/jitsi-meet)**  

  **The leading open-source video conferencing platform**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **25,000+ GitHub stars** . **WebRTC-based with no account required** — start a meeting instantly . **Scalable to hundreds of participants** with Jitsi Videobridge SFU . Features **screen sharing, recording, chat, hand raising, virtual backgrounds, and Etherpad integration** . **Self-hostable with Docker** or Debian packages . **No time limits, no participant caps** (limited by server capacity) . **The de facto open-source Zoom alternative** — used by universities, enterprises, and governments worldwide . **Best for general-purpose video meetings** .



- **[BigBlueButton](https://github.com/bigbluebutton/bigbluebutton)**  

  **The leading open-source platform for online teaching and training**, LGPL-3.0 licensed with **8,000+ GitHub stars** . **Built for education** — whiteboard, breakout rooms, polling, emoji reactions, and **LTI integration with Moodle** . **The gold standard for virtual classrooms** — designed for teaching interaction, not just meetings . **Self-hosted with Docker** . **Best for educational institutions and training organizations** .



- **[La Suite Meet](https://github.com/suitenumerique/meet)**  

  **Modern open-source video conferencing app powered by LiveKit**, MIT licensed with **2,392+ GitHub stars** . **Built with Django and React** by the French government's digital suite . **HD video calls, screen sharing, and chat** . **Positioned as a sovereign European alternative** for public sector and organizations wanting modern UX with LiveKit's distributed architecture . **Best for public sector and European organizations** .



- **[Element (Matrix)](https://github.com/element-hq/element-web)**  

  **Enterprise-grade messaging and collaboration built on Matrix**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **12,000+ GitHub stars** . **End-to-end encryption by default** with voice/video calling via WebRTC . **Federated architecture** — connect with other Matrix homeservers . **Bridges to Slack, Teams, Discord, and IRC** . **The most secure open-source communication platform** — used by governments, militaries, and privacy-critical organizations . **Best for maximum security and federation** .



- **[Nextcloud Talk](https://github.com/nextcloud/spreed)**  

  **Video conferencing and chat integrated with Nextcloud**, AGPL licensed . **Uses Janus SFU via High Performance Backend** for scale . **Integrates with Nextcloud files, calendar, and contacts** . **SIP integration via Talk SIP bridge** . **Best for teams already using Nextcloud** .



- **[edumeet](https://github.com/edumeet/edumeet)**  

  **Open-source video conferencing for research and education**, now self-sustaining under the Commons Conservancy . **Browser-based with no client installation** . **Supports federated architecture** — NRENs can share distributed resources . **Deployed across European research networks** including GARR (Italy), PCSS (Poland), and Helmholtz Cloud (Germany) . **Best for research and education institutions** .



- **[Galene](https://github.com/jech/galene)**  

  **Lightweight, easy-to-deploy video conferencing server**, MIT licensed . **Requires moderate server resources** — Go-based . **The simplest self-hosted option** for small teams wanting minimal operational overhead . **Best for small teams and low-resource deployments** .



- **[MiroTalk P2P](https://github.com/mirotalk/mirotalk)**  

  **Simple, secure, fast real-time video conferences up to 4K/60fps**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **Compatible with all browsers and platforms** . **Peer-to-peer architecture** — no SFU required for small calls . **Also available as MiroTalk SFU** for scalable conferences and **MiroTalk C2C** for embeddable cam-to-cam calls . **Best for quick, private video calls** .



- **[plugNmeet](https://github.com/mynaparrot/plugNmeet-server)**  

  **Scalable and high-performance web conferencing system**, Docker/Go-based . **Built on LiveKit with a focus on embedding and API-driven integration** . **Best for developers building custom conferencing into applications** .



- **[OpenVidu](https://github.com/OpenVidu/openvidu)**  

  **Open-source WebRTC platform for video conferencing**, Apache-2.0 licensed . **Build custom video applications with SDKs for JavaScript, React, Angular, Vue, iOS, Android, and Flutter** . **OpenVidu Meet** provides a ready-to-use Zoom-like application . **Best for developers building custom video apps** .



- **[LiveKit](https://github.com/livekit/livekit)**  

  **Open-source WebRTC platform for real-time video, audio, and data**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **10,000+ GitHub stars** . **Scalable SFU architecture** — used by La Suite Meet and many commercial platforms . **SDKs for all major platforms** . **Best for building scalable video applications** .



- **[Janus WebRTC Server](https://github.com/meetecho/janus-gateway)**  

  **General-purpose WebRTC server with plugin architecture**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Supports VideoRoom, VideoCall, SIP gateway, and streaming** . **Powers Nextcloud Talk's High Performance Backend** . **Best for flexible, multi-purpose WebRTC deployments** .



- **[mediasoup](https://github.com/versatica/mediasoup)**  

  **High-performance SFU library for WebRTC**, ISC licensed . **C++ core with Node.js signaling** . **The engine behind BigBlueButton** . **Best for building custom WebRTC applications** .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Apache OpenMeetings** — Web conferencing with whiteboard, chat, and recording, Apache-2.0 licensed .

- **Talk (Nextcloud)** — Already listed. **Integrated with Nextcloud** .

- **Spreed** — Nextcloud Talk's backend .

- **Jami** — Peer-to-peer video calling with no server required, GPL licensed .

- **Linphone** — Open-source SIP softphone with video, GPL licensed .

- **TrueConf Server Free** — Free video conferencing for up to 10 users (not fully open-source) .

- **Wire** — Secure collaboration (open-source client, discontinued enterprise) .

- **Tox** — Peer-to-peer encrypted messaging and video .

- **SimpleX** — Private messaging with video calls .

- **Briar** — Peer-to-peer messaging and video for activists .



**Frameworks for building custom video conferencing solutions**: Combine **Jitsi Meet** for general-purpose meetings with the fastest deployment path . Use **BigBlueButton** for education and training with LMS integration . Deploy **La Suite Meet** for modern UX with LiveKit's distributed architecture . Choose **Element (Matrix)** for maximum security and federation . Use **Nextcloud Talk** for integration with existing Nextcloud infrastructure . For building custom video applications: **LiveKit** for cloud-native scale , **mediasoup** for high-performance SFU , **Janus** for plugin flexibility , **OpenVidu** for rapid development with SDKs . Note that true enterprise video conferencing with global infrastructure, managed SLAs, and AI features (Zoom, Teams, Webex) remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong meeting, messaging, and WebRTC foundations that require integration for complete business communication.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Video conferencing platforms handle sensitive communications. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, TURN server configuration for NAT traversal, and compliance with data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA).

- **Bandwidth costs scale quadratically** with SFU deployments — 10 participants with cameras means 10 streams in and 90 streams out . Plan infrastructure accordingly.

- **TURN servers (Coturn)** are essential for NAT traversal — without them, calls fail for users behind symmetric NAT or corporate firewalls .

- **E2E encryption has trade-offs** — Element's default encryption means lost keys cannot be recovered, and server-side features like recording and moderation are limited . Jitsi provides E2E encryption as an option but it disables some features .

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong meeting, messaging, and WebRTC foundations, but **global infrastructure, managed SLAs, and AI features** remain primarily commercial offerings.
